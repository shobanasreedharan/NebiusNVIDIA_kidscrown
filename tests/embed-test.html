KidsCrown embed test

Paste the link to a video you know allows embedding (a video from a large kids' channel usually does). Then load it and watch for the things listed at the bottom.

A. Standard embed

B. Locked-down embed

Same player, but the page forbids it from opening new windows or navigating away.

What to look for, on each player

  1. Does it show the video and play when you press play?
  2. Tap the video title or the YouTube logo. Does it leave this page, open a new tab, or do nothing?
  3. Let the video finish (or skip near the end). Does it offer other videos to tap?
  4. Is there an error message? (For example "Error 153".)
  5. Does it work in the Chrome settings that allow only approved sites, with this page approved?
